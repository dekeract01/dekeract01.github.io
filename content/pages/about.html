--- title: About ---

I am current working as a modelling and simulations engineer, and I recently completed a PhD at the University of Southampton, where I studied laminar-turbulent transition in hypersonic flows. These days I work on scientific computing and numerical methods, and I love finding elegant ways to solve hard problems. I've also contributed to a few open source projects along the way. This site is where I share my research, projects and explorations.

I'm a keen advocate for STEM outreach and volunteered with the Royal Aeronautical Society for many years. Beyond that, you'll find me at my local parkrun, out on the bike, on a hike somewhere, losing hours to chess.com, or on the tennis court. Hopefully something here sparks your curiosity!
